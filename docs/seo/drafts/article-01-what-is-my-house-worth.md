## Why "what is my house worth?" is the right first question

Most people who ask us for an appraisal aren't ready to sell. They're weighing up a move, thinking about a renovation, or simply want to know where they stand. That's exactly the right time to ask. A realistic figure now lets you plan the timing, decide whether any work is worth doing, and avoid the two common traps: an inflated number designed to win a listing, and a rating valuation that's years out of date.

## What a property appraisal actually is

A property appraisal is a licensed salesperson's evidence-based estimate of the price your home should achieve in the current market. It's free, it takes about 45 minutes at the property, and there is no obligation to list.

It is not a registered valuation. A registered valuation is a paid, formal report from a registered valuer, and it's what a bank will usually ask for when you borrow. And it is not your council rating valuation (RV), which is set periodically for rates purposes and can sit well above or below what buyers are actually paying.

## What we base the number on

Recent comparable sales, first and foremost. In Palmerston North that means sales in your suburb, not a city-wide average: Hokowhitu, Kelvin Grove, Terrace End, Highbury, Takaro, Awapuni and Milson each have their own buyer pool and price behaviour. In Feilding, Ashhurst and the wider Manawatū it means the town or village, adjusted for section, condition and position.

Then the details that move the number: section size, garaging, sun and shelter, school zones, the state of the kitchen and bathrooms, and what's currently on the market competing with you. We show you the sales we used and the adjustments we made, so you understand the range rather than just receiving a figure.

## What happens at a Team Toner appraisal

Both of us come. You get two experienced agents looking at the same property, agreeing a range we're prepared to stand behind, and explaining the evidence behind it.

We'll walk through the home and the section, talk about your plans and timeframe, and follow up with the written range and comparable sales. If we think a different method of sale or a later date would get you more, we'll say so.
