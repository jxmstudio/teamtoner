## Why Ashhurst sells differently

Ashhurst sits 14 km north-east of Palmerston North's city centre, at the foot of the Ruahine Range and the western end of the Manawatū Gorge. It's part of Palmerston North City, but it doesn't feel like a suburb, and buyers don't shop it like one. They come for village life, bigger sections and a real community, and they compare your home with other Ashhurst homes first and with the city second.

That's why selling here takes local knowledge. A city-wide average tells you very little about what an Ashhurst buyer will pay for your home. What matters is what similar Ashhurst homes have sold for recently, what's on the market alongside yours, and how well your home answers the questions Ashhurst buyers ask.

## Who is buying in Ashhurst

Ashhurst School takes children from Year 1 right through to Year 8, the Ashhurst Domain and its lookout over the Te Āpiti wind farm are on the doorstep, and the city is an easy drive away. That combination draws buyers from three directions:

- Locals moving within the village — trading up to a bigger home or section, or down to something easier to look after
- Palmerston North families who want more land, a village school and a commute they can live with
- Buyers moving to the region for work who want space and a slower pace without being far from the city

## What buyers are paying in Ashhurst

We don't quote sale prices in articles like this one. Prices move, and every home is different, so a figure in an article would be out of date or wrong for your home before long. The most reliable guide to what buyers are paying is the most recent sales of homes like yours — which is exactly what an appraisal is built on.

You can see the homes we've sold in Ashhurst recently on our [Ashhurst area page](/suburbs/ashhurst#recent-sales). When we appraise an Ashhurst home, we compare it with those and other recent local sales, then adjust for the things that make the biggest difference here:

- Section size and usability — flat, sunny, fenced land is a big part of why people buy in Ashhurst
- Garaging and shedding for vehicles, trailers, boats and hobbies
- Sun, shelter and how the home handles the wind off the Gorge
- Age, insulation and heating, especially in older homes
- The condition of the kitchen, bathroom, roof and exterior
- Outlook and position on the street

## How to prepare your Ashhurst home for sale

Most of what helps an Ashhurst home sell well is simple, inexpensive work done before the photos are taken. Buyers here walk the section as carefully as they walk the house, so the outside counts as much as the inside.

Big renovations just before selling rarely pay for themselves. Ask us before you start anything major and we'll tell you honestly whether Ashhurst buyers are likely to pay for it. In the meantime, these are the jobs that usually do pay:

- Tidy the section: mow, edge, trim back overgrown planting and clear anything stored against the fences
- Make the garage and shed usable — buyers want to see the space, not your stored belongings
- Fix the small things buyers notice: sticking doors, dripping taps, missing light bulbs, a tired front door
- Gather your paperwork: building consents, code compliance certificates, insulation and heating details, and records of recent maintenance
- If you're buying as well as selling, talk to us early about how to line the two up

## Choosing how to sell

Ashhurst homes sell by asking price, deadline sale, auction and negotiation, and the right method depends on your home and the buyer interest we expect. A well-presented family home with broad appeal can suit a deadline sale or auction that brings buyers to a decision; a home with a narrower audience may do better with a price that helps the right buyer find it. We'll recommend a method at the appraisal and explain why.

Whichever method you choose, the marketing has to reach Palmerston North and out-of-town buyers as well as locals. Ours includes professional photography, aerial photography — nothing shows off an Ashhurst section like it — premium placement on the major property portals, signage, video and social marketing, all with No Upfront Costs.
